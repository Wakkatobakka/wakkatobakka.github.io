    04 / Bridges Before You Played Them

    The fun part was
    getting there.

    The failed builds. The strange fixes.

    Snowboarding put me inside Cloud’s chest. Once we sorted that out, he was upside down. Resident Evil 4 gave me a working gun and knife with no visible Leon. Of course I ran around until I got killed.

    I went back through the work and put together 52 histories from my private bridge work, with screenshots and development receipts. What fixed one game often became the missing piece for another. That larger pile became LEGION.

    These are the development stories. The current public playable lineup lives with Omni Player.

    06 / Something to read

    RIPPLE

    The Living Archive

    Logs, incidents, and observations.
    Five public records.

    This started with me asking AI for Mass Effect-style codex entries to listen to while mowing. It grew into a fictional universe told through incident reports, correspondence, and stories. Cosmic horror, weird humor, and occasional kindness.

    Eventually, it was scattered across old chats and files. I wanted RIPPLE back. So I dug through it with AI’s help, recovered what I could, and built somewhere to keep writing.

    A human/AI project by Wakka. Still being written.

    07 / Hey, I'm Wakka
    “Okay, but why can't we make it work?”

    Bridge dev / professional maker-of-weird-shit.

    I like taking software that was considered dead or obsolete and figuring out how to get it running anyway. I work with AI by directing, testing, and iterating, around my job and family.

    I've started calling the wider collection Legion. Beyond the public Omni catalog, there's a growing private testing pile, including N-Gage bridges. I'm working on playability, consistent controls, and making future updates easier to share.

    Mostly, I just think old things are neat. Getting other people playing them is part of the fun.

    This builds on the work of preservation, translation, and runtime projects. The N-Gage bridges use EKA2L1. Project pages carry the relevant credits and component notices.
